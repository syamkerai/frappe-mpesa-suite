# Round 4 verification, reads group: K3, K5, K9, K12, K15

Fixes on branch r4-verify-reads (from payouts-r4 81cc821). User-visible behaviour changes:

- **K3. A default phone on hold is no longer passed over for the Cell Number.** With "pay only approved phones" off (Trident and Astral after the upgrade), an employee who has approved phone accounts is paid by B2C only to the one picked now: the default, or the only one that can be paid. When the default is on hold or blocked, Pay employee (phone) refuses with the reason ("Employee M-Pesa Account ... is on hold until ... It is the employee's default: pick another approved account to pay now"). Pay by M-Pesa on the list shows the same reason for that claim or advance, and Get References on a B2C Payment Disbursement leaves the number empty. An employee with several approved phones and no default is asked to make one the default. Before, the Employee's Cell Number was used, which is often the new number still in its hold period. An employee with no approved phone at all is still paid to the Cell Number while the setting is off.
- **K5. Salary rows are refused before the slip is read.** On a B2C Payment Disbursement, who may not see salaries (for example an Accounts Manager or Expense Approver with no HR role) now gets the same "salaries are shown only..." refusal for any Salary Slip row, whatever the amount. Before, the answer changed with the slip's outstanding ("Allocated Amount cannot be greater than Outstanding Amount", "has nothing left to pay"), so repeated saves could find an employee's unpaid net pay. Users who may see salaries get the same messages as before.
- **K9. A B2B draft names an invoice, order, claim or advance only for who may read it.** When a Purchase Invoice, Purchase Order, Expense Claim or Employee Advance is added to a draft Mpesa B2B Payment Request, or changed on it, the user must be able to read that document (ERPNext's and HRMS's rights, User Permissions and shares). The save is refused before anything of the document is read, so the draft shows neither its supplier or employee nor what is left to pay. For example, HR roles and Expense Approvers (who now prepare B2B requests) can no longer save a request for any Purchase Invoice by typing its name.
  - Saving a draft whose document did not change needs no read on it. An M-Pesa Payer or Manager who saves or sends a colleague's draft is not refused.
  - Purchase Orders: ERPNext v15 and v16 give Purchase Order read only to Stock User, Purchase User and Purchase Manager. Accounts User and Accounts Manager cannot read Purchase Orders by default. They may still name an order on a B2B request because they read Purchase Invoices. Their existing order-advance flow is unchanged.
  - Payment schedule requests and the send are unchanged: Pay Selected checked the preparer.
- **K12. Get References lists only what the user may read.** On a B2C Payment Disbursement, Get References now needs read on the DocType being paid (Expense Claim, Employee Advance, Purchase Invoice, Purchase Order or Salary Slip). It lists only the documents the user may read one by one. A line manager who is an Expense Approver, limited to their own Employee record, now sees only the claims HRMS lets them read (those of the employees their User Permission allows, and claims shared with them), not every unpaid claim and advance in the company with each employee's phone. An HR User, whom HRMS gives no read on Employee Advances, is refused Get References for advances. Purchase Orders follow the K9 rule: who reads Purchase Invoices (the Accounts roles) also lists orders.
  - Accounts User and Accounts Manager read no Expense Claims or Employee Advances in stock HRMS. They now get these references only once the site gives them that read, as the Pay buttons on the claim already required.
- **K15. A supplier's approved phones also bind hand-made B2C requests and retries.**
  - A B2C Disbursement Request made by hand for a supplier that has approved Phone (B2C) Supplier M-Pesa Accounts must go to one of them that can be paid now. It is checked on save and at the send, like a disbursement row. A supplier with no approved phone account is paid to any number, as before.
  - Retry (on the request or on its disbursement) of a failed supplier payout checks the approved account its row named again. It refuses when that account was cancelled (for example a stolen SIM), even if it was the supplier's only approved phone. It also refuses when the account is on hold or blocked, or when its number is no longer the payout's. A payout whose row named no account is checked against the supplier's approved phones, if it has any now.
