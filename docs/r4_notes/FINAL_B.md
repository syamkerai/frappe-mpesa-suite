# Round 4 final review, area B: salaries, schedules, payee accounts

Fixes on branch r4-final-b (from payouts-r4 56f8e74). User-visible behaviour changes:

- An approved Supplier or Employee M-Pesa Account can now be cancelled after it was paid. Before, Frappe refused the cancel while any submitted B2B request (Paid, Failed or Unknown) or a submitted Expense Claim's M-Pesa Payees row named it ("Cannot delete or cancel because ... is linked with Mpesa B2B Payment Request ..."), the same on v15 and v16. The payouts and claims keep the destination they copied, and every send checks the account again (a cancelled one is refused).
- Cancelling an account from its form no longer offers "Cancel All" for the B2B requests or Expense Claims that name it.
- An amended account starts unblocked: Amend copies every field of the cancelled account, including its block (Blocked: Approve Again, Name Did Not Match) and its last payment. The new draft now clears those, so "cancel, amend and approve again" really unblocks an account (it used to stay blocked after the new approval).
- A B2B request that names no account (and the prefill on a Purchase Invoice, Purchase Order, Salary Slip, Expense Claim or Employee Advance) goes to the party's default account only. When the default cannot be paid now (on hold, blocked), the request is refused with that reason ("... is on hold until ... It is the supplier's default: pick another approved account to pay now"). It is no longer filled with another approved account without a word. The payer can still pick another approved account. With no default of the right kind, the only payable one is still picked (for example, the one bank account beside a default phone).
- On every migrate (so on the round 4 upgrade), approved accounts that never had a second named approver are blocked (Blocked: Approve Again) until they are cancelled, amended and approved by a named M-Pesa Manager. That means accounts approved by the person who created them or entered their details, and accounts approved with the shared Administrator login while "refuse Administrator payouts" is on (the default). Trident's MSA-00002 (handover F3) is one. Each blocked account gets a Comment, and the Notify Role gets one alert per DocType with a count (no names for employee accounts). An account that is blocked already is left alone.
- A bank paybill that changes back to its old number now unblocks only the accounts its own change blocked, not accounts blocked for another reason.
- M-Pesa Payment Schedule: who may not see salaries (for example an Accounts User or Accounts Manager without an HR role) can no longer add, change, keep or remove a salary line. A salary line is an employee line for a Salary Slip, or for no claim or advance. The save is refused before the slip or the employee's account is read, so the message names no amount and no bank account or phone. Supplier lines are unchanged. Schedules made by the Payroll Entry and Salary Slip buttons are unchanged (those builders already check salary visibility).
- HRMS's Make Bank Entry on a Payroll Entry, or any Bank or Cash Entry made by hand that references a payroll, is refused while M-Pesa pays salaries of that payroll. That covers payouts that are Paid (booked or not), on their way, or still planned on a schedule that is not closed. HRMS's entry pays the net pay of every slip and does not know what M-Pesa paid. A payroll is paid either through M-Pesa or through Make Bank Entry, or it is split into separate Payroll Entries. When Payroll Settings books payroll per employee, only the rows of the employees M-Pesa pays are refused, so a bank entry made by hand for the other employees goes through. The message names the payouts only to who may see salaries (a count otherwise). The suite's own salary entries are not affected.
- The salary Journal Entry booked for a schedule's B2C or B2B salary now puts the Payroll Entry's cost center on the M-Pesa (credit) row too. Before, only the payroll payable row had it. Without one on the Payroll Entry, both rows take the paying shortcode's cost center (Mpesa Settings), else the company's default (handover F5, as for every other payout entry). The round-off row keeps the company's round-off cost center.
- An Expense Claim or Employee Advance form now counts its M-Pesa payouts in Connections for a user who reads that DocType by a role of their own (for example Accounts User plus Expense Approver), even without salary visibility (plan C.8). Before, the M-Pesa group showed no count to them. Salary Slip, Payroll Entry, Employee and Employee M-Pesa Account counts are unchanged.
- A Transaction Status query about a B2B payout of an Expense Claim or Employee Advance is no longer treated as salary data: who reads the claim (or advance) sees it, like the request itself. A query about a salary B2B, or about an employee payment for no document, stays hidden.
- Versions (track changes) of salary records are now hidden like the records from who may not see salaries, including System Manager, which Frappe lets read every Version. That covers salary schedules, B2C payouts and requests to employees, B2B salaries and their status queries. Versions of every Employee M-Pesa Account are hidden too. Versions of supplier payouts, claim payouts and other documents are listed as before, and each form's own timeline is unchanged.
- The Supplier M-Pesa Account and Employee M-Pesa Account lists show indicators: Blocked: Approve Again and Blocked: Name Did Not Match (red), Default (green) and Approved (blue), next to Draft and Cancelled.

Verified, no change needed (integration to-do and review items in this area):
- Employee M-Pesa Account has no System Manager DocPerm (only M-Pesa Manager, HR Manager and HR User).
- M-Pesa Payment Schedule: System Manager has read only. Create and write belong to Accounts Manager, Accounts User and M-Pesa Manager (all PREPARE_ROLES), and the builders call require(PREPARE_ROLES). Salary lines are now also checked for salary visibility (above).
- The "third-party account" warning on a bank account already shows on save of the draft (validate_account runs on every draft save). Showing it in the Approve summary is send_confirm's job (area C).
