# Final review, area A: claims, advances, merchant and casual payees, the B2C and B2B payout engine

## Behaviour changes for users
- An Expense Claim's approved merchant and casual payee lines that are not paid yet (Draft, Failed, or waiting in a draft B2C batch) are held back from what the claim has left for its employee. Pay employee (phone), Pay employee (bank / paybill / till), the list's Pay by M-Pesa, the headline, a B2B request to the employee and the B2C send and retry of the employee's own payout all offer or allow only the rest. The headline shows the held amount as "for payees". Pay Merchants and Payees, Retry Failed Payees and a payee's own payout count the lines they pay as their own share.
- The B2C send (Send via M-Pesa on a B2C Payment Disbursement) and the retry of a failed B2C payout of an Expense Claim or Employee Advance take off draft Payment and Journal Entries allocated to it, as Prepare and B2B already did. A claim with HRMS's Make Payment Entry left as a draft for the whole amount can no longer be paid again by B2C.
- A failed payout (B2B request, B2C disbursement or B2C request) and a draft B2B request no longer stop an Expense Claim or Employee Advance being cancelled. Cancel and amend is how a mistyped phone, till or account is corrected. A payout that is paid or on its way still stops the cancel.
- A draft B2B request or B2C batch that a claim's payee line names can be deleted, and a manager can cancel an Unknown request a line names. The line keeps the payout's name and the approved supplier account's name as plain text, linked from the line's notes on the claim form. An amended claim no longer fails with "Could not find Payout" or "Cannot link cancelled document".
- A payout of a payee line must pay the whole line. A request or row for less (or more) than the line is refused.
- A Paybill payee line whose paybill is any M-Pesa Bank Paybill row is refused, including a row shipped disabled ("switched off ... an M-Pesa Manager checks its paybill and enables it"). A Paybill line whose account number is an employee's bank account number (the Employee's Bank A/C No. or an Employee M-Pesa bank account) is refused as the claimant's own or an employee's bank account.
- Approving (submitting) an Expense Claim that has payee lines is refused for the shared Administrator login while M-Pesa Suite Settings refuses it for payouts. A rejection is not refused.
- Get References for Salary Slips on a B2C Payment Disbursement is refused to anyone who may not see salaries, such as an Accounts Manager or Accounts User without an HR role. It also needs read on the Payroll Entry, which HRMS gives to HR Manager only, and lists only the slips the user may read. Saving a draft disbursement with a Salary Slip row needs the same rights. The send itself is not affected.
- A B2B request to an employee for a Salary Slip, or for no Expense Claim or Employee Advance, can be made or changed only by someone who may see salaries, and a named slip must be readable. Before, an Accounts User alone could save one, read the slip's net pay from the refusal and see the employee's bank account copied into the draft. Payment schedule requests and the send are not affected.
- A B2C Disbursement Request made by hand (over the API) can no longer pay a Salary Slip. One to an employee for no document is sent by an M-Pesa Manager only, as a B2B request with no document is.
- A B2C Payment Disbursement row paying a supplier that has approved Phone (B2C) Supplier M-Pesa Accounts pays only one of them, and only while it can be paid (not on hold or blocked). Get References fills in the default one. The row names the account, and the name M-Pesa paid is checked against it. A supplier with no approved phone account is still paid to its Contact's phone.
- A B2C payout of an Expense Claim or Employee Advance that the document can no longer take in full (paid another way since the send) is booked with the document taking what HRMS says it still owes. The rest stays unallocated on the employee's Payment Entry, with a Comment on the payout and an alert to the Notify Role ("Check that the employee was not paid twice"). Before, ERPNext refused the entry on every sweep for 30 days. When the document can take nothing, the payout's note says where its unallocated entry is.
- Make Entry on a paid B2B request: booking one paid after its request was cancelled, and moving a paid payout to another Salary Slip, Expense Claim, Employee Advance, invoice, order or supplier, need a named M-Pesa Manager. The shared Administrator login is refused. A payout of a claim's payee line or of a payment schedule line is never moved off its document.
- With M-Pesa Suite Settings > Expense Claim Workflow States That May Be Paid set, an Employee Advance under a Workflow of its own takes only the listed states its Workflow has. A site listing its claim states no longer refuses every advance. An Expense Claim takes the whole list, as before.
- The M-Pesa Connections of an Expense Claim and an Employee Advance count their payouts for anyone who reads the claim (or advance) and the payout DocType, as the payouts themselves are shown (they are not salary data). Before, they were hidden from users who may not see salaries.
